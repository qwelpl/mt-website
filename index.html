Hard work as a daily habit, not a punishment

We split our space into small classrooms on purpose — every student sits within arm's reach of the instructor, and gets the individual attention they'd otherwise wait weeks for at a bigger center.

We don't think teaching and mentoring are separate jobs. Our instructors build real relationships with students, which makes the daily grind of practice sets and vocabulary drills something students choose to stick with rather than resent. When a classroom's culture treats hard work as normal, the results — and the enjoyment — tend to follow.

What we teach

Group & private tutorials

Common Core through AP · English composition

Our English program builds the reading and writing skills that carry through every other subject: grammar, vocabulary, essay composition, reading comprehension, and in-class discussion, with samples sent home so parents can see the actual work.

Our Common Core 7 and 8 math sequence builds the algebra foundation students need before Algebra 1 — especially translating word problems into equations, a skill most students never get formal practice with at school. Private one-on-one sessions are available for any subject when a student needs pacing a group class can't offer.

Standardized test prep

SAT · SAT II Math, Chemistry, Biology, Physics · AP

SAT prep covers Critical Reading, Writing, and Math using our own "information box" method for breaking down word problems, plus weekly mock tests scored and reported back like the real thing. SAT II Math II is our strongest subject test — students walk in already familiar with the exact question types they'll see.

Every class runs on weekly homework, quizzes, and progress reports emailed to parents — SAT I classes get at least two reports a week — so a struggling topic gets caught long before test day, not after.

College application services

Essay coaching · one-on-one counseling · UC GPA planning

Our counselors work one-on-one on personal statements and supplemental essays until they sound like the student, not a template — most well-prepared students only need a few hours of editing once the ideas are there. We also help families understand mechanics that colleges rarely explain clearly, like how the UC system calculates GPA from "a-g" courses in grades 10 and 11.

Register early and use the Early Bird discount on one-on-one counseling — the first meeting is scheduled within seven business days of registration, or the service is fully refundable.
